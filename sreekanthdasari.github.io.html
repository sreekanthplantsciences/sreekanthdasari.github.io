About

I am a Scientist in Plant Physiology at the ICAR-Directorate of Weed Research (DWR) in Jabalpur, part of the Indian Council of Agricultural Research. I joined in 2020, and my work covers research, teaching and extension.

My research asks how elevated CO₂, rising temperature and drought change the competition between crops and weeds, and what that does to herbicide performance. I look at photosynthesis, antioxidant systems, yield traits and gene expression, working across rice, wheat, greengram, mungbean and soybean.

A second strand of my work finds how weeds resist herbicides, including target-site mutations, cytochrome P450-mediated resistance and biochemical detoxification, using de novo transcriptome analysis. A third uses CRISPR/Cas genome editing, along with EMS mutagenesis, to generate herbicide-tolerant crops. Outside the lab I help build tools for farmers and students, including the Weedseed GURU mobile app.

I completed my Ph.D. in Botany at Osmania University, Hyderabad (2020), and from 2026 to 2027 I am an INSA Visiting Scientist at CSIR-IHBT, Palampur.

Research

Crop–weed interaction under climate change

Open-top chamber and controlled-environment experiments show that elevated CO₂ and temperature intensify weed competition and reduce rice and wheat yields. We identified the main weed threats and how each responds.

Agricultural Research 2025 · Indian Journal of Weed Science 2022, 2023, 2024

Drought, weeds and herbicide efficacy

Physiological, biochemical and molecular studies of rice, wheat and greengram growing with weeds under drought, and how drought reduces herbicide efficacy.

Plant and Soil 2024 · Scientific Reports 2025 · J. Plant Growth Regulation 2025 · Functional Plant Biology 2026

Herbicide resistance and transcriptomics

De novo transcriptome analysis of Commelina spp. and Echinochloa colona to uncover resistance to imazethapyr, and of Parthenium hysterophorus to understand its drought tolerance.

Best oral presentation, ISWS Biennial Conference 2024

Herbicide-tolerant crops

Standardised EMS mutagenesis in maize and soybean, screened 104 soybean and 100 maize genotypes for tolerance to glyphosate and glufosinate-ammonium, and designed CRISPR/Cas constructs for herbicide tolerance.

J. Plant Biochemistry and Biotechnology 2025 · Springer protocol chapter 2026

Tools for weed identification and biocontrol

Lead developer of the Weedseed GURU mobile app and of weed seed identification kits for Kharif and Rabi weeds. Associate developer of a biocontrol technology for Salvinia molesta using Cyrtobagous salviniae.

ICAR-certified products and technologies, 2024

Frameworks and reviews

Conceptual frameworks for direct-seeded rice traits, nanoherbicides, and phytohormones for salinity tolerance, each with a supporting publication.

Rice Science 2024 · Plant Nano Biology 2026 · Plant Stress 2026
